16 Metrics

Entry Gate

Buy when setup scores 60+, price is within 1% of target, volume is strong, and market conditions are good.

Triple-Constraint Sizing

Risk 0.5% of account per trade. No single position over 10%. Partial positions when cash is limited. Max 10 positions per sector.

Circuit Breakers

Stop after 10 trades in one day. Halt trading if daily loss exceeds 2%. Re-entry cooldown after losses.

Exit Ladder

Move stop to breakeven at +1R profit. Trail EMA10 at +1.5R, EMA21 at +3R. Hard stop protects against major drops.
